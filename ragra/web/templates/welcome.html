Ragra

Never miss a Google Classroom deadline. Ragra reads your classes with your permission, shows what is due, and reminds you before each deadline.

Sign in with Google

What it does

  • Lists your assignments, announcements and class materials in one place.
  • Emails you a reminder a week, five days, two days and an hour before something is due.
  • Adds your deadlines to Google Calendar.
  • Only asks for read-only access to Classroom.

Good to know

  • Google may warn that the app is unverified. The help page shows what to click.
  • You can delete your account, or revoke access in your Google account, at any time.
