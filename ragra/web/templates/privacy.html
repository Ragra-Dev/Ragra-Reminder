Privacy policy

Ragra is a deadline-reminder service for Google Classroom. It reads your classes and assignments, and reminds you before they are due. This page explains what it collects, why, and how to remove it. Last updated: 2026-10-24.

What Ragra collects

  • Your Google account identity: your name, email address and Google account identifier, used to sign you in and to keep your data separate from everyone else's.
  • Google Classroom data you authorise: the names of your classes, and the titles, descriptions, due dates, announcements, class materials and your own submission status. Ragra only requests read-only access.
  • Google Calendar access you authorise: Ragra creates and updates deadline events on your calendar. It does not use your other events for anything else.
  • A sign-in cookie so the site remembers you for up to 7 days.

How it is used

Only to show your deadlines, send you reminders by email, and add deadlines to your calendar. Ragra does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with anyone, except the hosting provider that runs the service and the email provider that sends your reminders.

Ragra's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Humans do not read your Google data, except where you ask for support, where it is needed for security or to comply with the law.

How it is stored

  • Your data is kept in a database on the server that runs the service.
  • Your Google access tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Connections use HTTPS.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: sign in, open Account and choose Delete account. Your data is removed immediately.
  • Revoke access: at myaccount.google.com/permissions you can remove Ragra's access to your Google account at any time.
