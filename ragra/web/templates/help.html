Getting started

It takes about two minutes. You need a Google account that is in at least one Google Classroom class.

1. Sign in

Click Sign in with Google and choose your account.

2. If Google says the app isn't verified

You may see "Google hasn't verified this app". That is expected while Ragra is small. Do not click the large Back to safety button, which cancels the sign-in. Click the small Advanced link, then Go to Ragra (unsafe).

3. Connect Classroom and Calendar

  1. Open Account and click Connect next to Classroom.
  2. On Google's permissions screen, tick every box, then click Continue.
  3. Do the same for Calendar.

4. Wait for the first sync

Ragra checks Classroom every couple of minutes. Your assignments appear on the Today page after the first check.

If something goes wrong

  • Nothing shows up: check that the Google account you signed in with is the one that is in your classes.
  • "Reconnect could not be completed": go back to Account and click Connect again, and tick every box. Don't reload the Google page.
  • Reminders stopped: your Google connection may have expired. Open Account and reconnect.
  • Remove everything: Account → Delete account, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
